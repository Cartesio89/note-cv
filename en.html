Personal projects

Projects I conceived and carried forward by directing development with AI tools (Claude): I define goals, rules and choices, then test and correct; the code is generated with AI.

Transferable skills

  • I always start from a real problem (my football group, my holiday home, my documents, my finances, my job search) and build the tool I need.
  • I direct development with AI: I define requirements and rules, test, correct and iterate. I do not hand-write the code, and I say so openly.
  • I care about privacy and costs: data in private repositories, minimal-permission tokens, row-level security, demos kept apart from real data.
  • I am persistent: I take projects beyond the prototype, through many versions (for example a personal finance app at version 6.3 and a job app of about 2,700 lines).
  • I reason analytically when I can: for example with a Monte Carlo simulation to balance a game.
  • I use these skills in my digital marketing work: SEO, structured data, creative formats, automation and data handling.

Five-a-side football site: Il Capodanno di Nainggolan

I designed and directed, with AI assistance, a web app for my football group (about 60 players): rankings, peer ratings, pair statistics and an internal fantasy football game.

Products and technologies: HTML/JavaScript, GitHub Pages with custom domain, Supabase (Postgres), PWA, Claude.

Key points:

  • I defined the business rules: ratings from 0 to 10 in 0.25 steps within 48 hours of the result; a fantasy game with a 15-player squad, 150 credits and prices that move with performance.
  • I set up different roles (president, player, guest), with a read-only guest link and tables that cannot be read directly from outside.
  • It is used by real people, not a demo.

Terrazza Galba website

Products and technologies: HTML/CSS/JavaScript, Netlify, JSON-LD structured data, sitemap and robots.txt, Google Search Console, content in a content.json file.

Key points:

Home documents management app

I built an app to store and find home documents (invoices, contracts, bills, works) that also works offline and syncs when the connection returns. I also created a demo with fictitious data.

Products and technologies: Plain JavaScript, IndexedDB, Service Worker, private GitHub repository as storage, PWA.

Key points:

  • I asked for offline handling with a sync queue and per-item conflict resolution, instead of an online-only app.
  • I kept the demo separate from my real data to protect the privacy of my documents.
